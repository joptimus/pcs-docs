# Personnel Qualification

Pro Captain Staffing maintains rigorous standards for all personnel to ensure the safety, professionalism, and quality of our operations. All employees must meet the following requirements and adhere to established operational guidelines.

## Regulatory Compliance

- **Comply with all U.S. Coast Guard regulations and standards.** Refer to [uscg.gov](https://www.uscg.gov) for current requirements and guidance.

## Certification Requirements

Personnel must hold a valid captain's certification or be actively pursuing one of the following credentials:

- Party Barge Operator
- Operator of Uninspected Passenger Vessels (OUPV)
- Master Captain

## Medical and Safety Certifications

- Maintain current CPR and First Aid certification at all times.

## Professional Standards

- Maintain punctuality for all assigned operations.
- Provide a safe, professional, and enjoyable experience for all passengers.
- Commit to ongoing professional development and skill enhancement.

## Operational Requirements

- Wear the approved uniform during all duty periods.
- Monitor VHF radio channels 16 and 13 while underway.
- Maintain proper decorum throughout the vessel, including removing footwear before entering the salon area.

## Incident Management

- Report any incidents, accidents, or safety concerns to proper authorities immediately.
- Notify Pro Captain Staffing management of all incidents and discrepancies as soon as possible.
